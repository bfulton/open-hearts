# Working on Open Hearts

Two rules from the owner, for anyone working in this repository, Claude
included.

- **Never send email.** Don't run anything that emails anyone, `./oh
  terms notify --apply` included. When an email is due, give the owner its
  exact text and the command, and let them send it.
- **Every notification is approved first.** Before anything a player or
  subscriber is shown or sent goes out (a notice or dialog in the app, an
  email, a summary in `site/ledger.json`), show the owner exactly what it
  says and how it looks: a screenshot, or the email's text. Then wait for
  their approval.
