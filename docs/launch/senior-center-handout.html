Open Hearts

Free on your phone, tablet or computer

The card game for four: you and three computer players. Try not to take hearts or the queen of spades. Lowest score wins.

Start playing

  1. Open the internet browser: Safari, Chrome or Edge.
  2. At the top, type this address, then press Go or Enter:
    [PLAY URL]
  3. Tap the gold button that says Play solo.
  4. At the start of a hand you pick 3 cards to give away: tap them, then tap Pass. Now you’re playing.

Tap a card you can’t play, and the game tells you why. Save the page as a bookmark to find it again.

It’s free

  • Playing alone is always free.
  • No ads. No sign-up.
  • No name, email or card needed to play alone.
  • “Live play” costs money. It is for playing people online. You don’t need it.

Make it bigger

  • Computer: hold Ctrl and press +. Mac: ⌘ and +. Ctrl and 0 puts it back.
  • Phone or tablet: find Zoom in the browser’s menu.
  • A tablet shows bigger cards than a phone.
