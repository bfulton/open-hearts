Support

How to get help, and answers to what people ask.

Getting help

  • ♠
    Something is broken Choose Tell us what's broken in the game's menu, or open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public.
  • ♥
    Anything private Email help@openhearts.game, or write to us at the address in the terms.

Questions

How do I play?

Open openhearts.game/play and tap Play solo. The rules are on the front page.

Is it in the App Store or Google Play?

Not yet. Add the page to your home screen to keep it handy; playing alone then works offline.

How do I subscribe?

Tap Subscribe under Play live. Payment is on Stripe's page. There is no account: the subscription belongs to the device, and to any you add.

How do I cancel?

Open Live play and choose Manage subscription. Other ways, and refunds, are in the terms.

How do I add another device?

On a subscribed device, open Live play and tap Add a device, then scan the code with the other device, or tap Send the link instead. A code works once, for ten minutes.

I lost my phone, or cleared my browser.

From another device on the subscription, add the new one and drop the lost one. If you have none, email us with the address you paid with.

Can I play with a screen reader, or bigger text?

Yes. In a browser, press Enable accessibility, the first button a screen reader finds. For bigger text, use the browser's zoom.

Can I pick the language?

Add ?lang=es (or pt, de, fr, en) to the game's address.

How do I know the deal is fair?

See how the deal works.

What happens to my data?
