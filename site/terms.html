The live-play subscription

What it includes

  • Being seated at a table with other subscribers who are waiting, with bots in the seats nobody takes.
  • Tables of your own, with a link to send to friends who also subscribe.
  • Rated games, and a rating worked out from results everyone at the table signs.
  • The services that make a table work: putting devices in touch with each other, passing traffic between them when they cannot reach each other directly, and jurors that keep a table going.
  • Up to five of your devices on one subscription, added and dropped by you.

Everyone at a live table has subscribed. Live play needs an internet connection and some of it runs through public servers that other people operate. We do our best to keep it working, but we cannot promise that it always will, or that a particular game will finish.

Price and length

A subscription lasts one year and costs $5.99 a year in US dollars on the web, paid up front, plus any tax shown at checkout. In the App Store or Google Play the price is the one the store shows you, in your currency, before you buy.

Automatic renewal

A subscription renews automatically every year, at the price then in force, and is charged to the same payment method, until you cancel it. If the price changes, the new price applies only from your next renewal, and the store, or for the web we, will tell you before it does.

  • App Store: payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current year, and your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before that.
  • Google Play: renewal and charging follow Google Play's rules, shown when you buy.
  • Web: the purchase is made through Stripe, and Stripe charges the card you gave it each year on the date you first subscribed.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal; live play keeps working until the end of the year you have paid for.

  • App Store: on your iPhone or iPad open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Open Hearts.
  • Google Play: in the Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  • Web: in the game, open Live play and choose Manage subscription, on any of the subscription's devices. That opens Stripe's billing page, where you can cancel, change the card, and see your receipts. If you no longer have any of those devices, write to us or call (details below) with the email address you paid with, and we will cancel it and confirm.

A refunded subscription stops being renewed, and live play on its devices ends when their current licence runs out, within about a month.

Add a second device to keep a way back, or contact us with the email address you paid with on the web, and we will help where we can.

At a live table

Your name, icon and picture, and the result of every live game you play, are shown to other players and published on public servers where anyone can read them and where they may be kept for good. Do not put anything in them that you would not want a stranger to see.

You agree not to:

  • use a name or picture that is hateful, sexual, violent, harassing or illegal, or that infringes someone else's rights;
  • pretend to be another person, or to be us;
  • harass, threaten or abuse other players;
  • cheat: share cards with another player, play several seats at one table, or alter the game to gain an advantage;
  • attack, overload or try to break the service, the relays, or other players' devices, or use them for anything but playing.

A player who stops playing can be booted by the table, and a bot takes the seat. A player who leaves a rated game finishes it last. You can hide another player's picture on your own device at any time.

To report a player or a picture, contact us (below). We may stop renewing live play for a device that breaks these rules, and if we do so without a good reason we will refund the unused part of a web subscription.
