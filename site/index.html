How Hearts works

  1. Four players, thirteen cards each.
  2. Pass three cards: left, right, across, then no pass.
  3. The two of clubs leads. Follow suit if you can.
  4. No points on the first trick, unless you have nothing else.
  5. Hearts can't be led until one has been played.
  6. Each heart is one point; the queen of spades is thirteen.
  7. Take all twenty-six to shoot the moon: everyone else gets them.
  8. Play to fifty or a hundred. Lowest score wins.
